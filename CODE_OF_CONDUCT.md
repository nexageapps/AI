# Code of Conduct

We want learning here to be welcoming to everyone, whatever their background or level.

**Expected:** be kind and patient; assume good faith; give specific, constructive feedback; credit others' work; keep discussion about the material.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling, sharing others' private information, or posting content you don't have the right to share.

**Academic honesty:** this material is for learning. Do not use it to submit work you did not do, or in any assessment where it is not permitted. See [documentation/ACADEMIC_INTEGRITY.md](./documentation/ACADEMIC_INTEGRITY.md).

**Enforcement:** maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions and may block people who breach these expectations. Report concerns by opening a private security/contact issue via the repository's contact options or the maintainer links in the README.
