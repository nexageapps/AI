## UoA Advanced AI Programmes Overview

The University of Auckland offers a structured pathway of postgraduate AI qualifications through the School of Computer Science. These programmes are designed for developers and researchers looking to deepen their expertise in machine learning and automated cognition.

> Source: [University of Auckland — AI Programmes](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/study/study-options/find-a-study-option/master-of-artificial-intelligence--mai-.html)

### Postgraduate Certificate in Artificial Intelligence (PGCertAI)

- **Points:** 60
- **Duration:** 1 semester (full-time)
- **Core courses:** COMPSCI 712, 713, 714, INFOSYS 703
- **Entry:** BSc or relevant undergraduate degree, GPA ≥ 4.0 in 60 pts above Stage II
- **2026 Fees (indicative):** ~NZ$5,936 (domestic) / ~NZ$27,743 (international)
- **More info:** [PGCertAI Programme Page](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/study/study-options/find-a-study-option/postgraduate-certificate-in-artificial-intelligence.html)

### Postgraduate Diploma in Artificial Intelligence (PGDipAI)

- **Points:** 120 (60 core + 60 elective)
- **Duration:** 1 year (full-time)
- **Core courses:** COMPSCI 712, 713, 714, INFOSYS 703
- **Electives from:** COMPSCI 703, 705, 720, 732, 734, 750–753, 760, 761, 762, 764, 765, 767, 769, 773, COMPSYS 726, 731, 732, and others
- **Entry:** BSc or relevant undergraduate degree, GPA ≥ 4.0 in 60 pts above Stage II
- **2026 Fees (indicative):** ~NZ$12,909 (domestic) / ~NZ$54,508 (international)
- **More info:** [PGDipAI Programme Page](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/study/study-options/find-a-study-option/postgraduate-diploma-in-artificial-intelligence.html)

### Master of Artificial Intelligence (MAI)

- **Points:** 120 or 180 (depending on pathway)
- **Duration:** 1–1.5 years (full-time)
- **120-point pathway:** Requires BAdvSci(Hons), BE, BE(Hons), or BSc(Hons) with GPA ≥ 4.0 in 60 pts above Stage III
- **180-point pathway:** Requires BSc with GPA ≥ 4.0 in 60 pts above Stage II
- **2026 Fees (indicative):** ~NZ$11,882–$12,909 (domestic) / ~NZ$54,508 (international)
- **More info:** [MAI Programme Page](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/study/study-options/find-a-study-option/master-of-artificial-intelligence--mai-.html)

## How Our Tutorials Map to UoA Programmes

### Recommended Study Path for PGCertAI Students

1. **Before Semester:** Work through Basic lessons B01–B05 for foundational understanding
2. **Alongside COMPSCI 713:** B01 (Symbolic Logic), plus the Wumpus World and KG Playground demos
3. **Alongside COMPSCI 714:** B05, B05a, B05b (Neural Networks), B09 (CNNs), B11 (Transformers), plus live demos
4. **Alongside COMPSCI 712:** A14 (Responsible AI), I14 (Explainable AI), E12 (Privacy-Preserving Learning)
5. **Alongside INFOSYS 703:** A10 (ML Pipelines), I15 (MLOps), A15 (Production Case Studies)

### Recommended Study Path for PGDipAI / MAI Students

All of the above, plus:

6. **For COMPSCI 760:** I01, I02, I10, I12, E10 (Advanced ML topics)
7. **For COMPSCI 761:** B01, A02, A03, E04 (Advanced AI topics)
8. **For COMPSCI 762:** B02–B04, B07–B08, I03, I11 (ML Foundations)
9. **For COMPSYS 721:** B09–B10, I06–I08, A04–A05 (Deep Learning)
10. **For COMPSCI 703:** I05, A01, E07–E08, I13 (Generalisation)
